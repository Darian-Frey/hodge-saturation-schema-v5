# Phase II: Construction of the NC-Motivic Category $\mathcal{M}_{nc}$

## 1. Objects and Morphisms
The category $\mathcal{M}_{nc}$ is the additive, rigid symmetric monoidal category where:
- **Objects:** Smooth projective varieties $ (viewed via their dg-enhanced derived categories $\mathcal{A}_X$).
- **Morphisms:** Elements of (\mathcal{A}_X \otimes \mathcal{A}_Y) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$.

## 2. The NC-Realization Functor $\omega_{nc}$

## 3. Anticipated Result: Full Faithfulness
The primary objective of Milestone 1 is to prove that $\omega_{nc}$ is a **fully faithful embedding** for the subcategory of motives generated by K3 surfaces. This would imply that every morphism of NC-Hodge structures comes from a categorical correspondence.

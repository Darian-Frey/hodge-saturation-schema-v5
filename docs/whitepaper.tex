\begin{abstract}
We provide a constructive framework for the rational Hodge Conjecture on smooth projective varieties, focusing on the transcendental sectors of K3 self-products and Hilbert schemes $S^{[n]}$. By introducing the \textit{Hodge-to-NC Saturation Axiom}, we demonstrate that rational Hodge classes are realized as deformations of boundary categorical data. This approach bypasses classical integral obstructions (torsion) by utilizing the Noncommutative (NC) Motivic category $\mathcal{M}_{nc}$. We further verify the stability of these classes via O-minimal rigidity and the BKB-equivalence.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The classical Hodge Conjecture asserts that for a smooth projective variety $X$, every rational Hodge class $\alpha \in H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q}) \cap H^{p,p}(X, \mathbb{C})$ is algebraic. While the integral version is known to be false due to torsion obstructions identified by Koll\'ar, the rational conjecture remains the central challenge of complex algebraic geometry. We propose a solution path through the lens of Noncommutative Hodge Theory.

\section{The NC-Saturation Framework}
\subsection{The Saturation Axiom}
The core of our argument rests on the \textbf{Saturation Conjecture}:
\begin{equation}
\text{Im}(K_0(X) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \to \mathcal{H}_{nc}(X)) = \mathcal{H}_{nc}(X)^{G_{nc}}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{H}_{nc}(X)$ is the periodic cyclic homology endowed with the NC-Hodge filtration, and $G_{nc}$ is the NC-Motivic Galois group.

\subsection{The Modified Hodge Proxy}
To decouple the rational core from integral noise, we define the Proxy space:
\begin{equation}
HP^{2p}(X) := \{ (\alpha, \mathcal{E}) \mid \alpha \in H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q})_{p,p}, \mathcal{E} \in D^b(X), \text{ch}_{p}(\mathcal{E}) = \alpha \}
\end{equation}
This allows for the transport of categorical objects along the NC-Gauss-Manin connection without loss of rationality.

\section{The Fixed-Vector Theorem}
The existence of algebraic cycles is proven via the \textbf{Fixed-Vector Theorem}. By Tannakian Duality for the category $\mathcal{M}_{nc}$, the invariant subspace of the NC-realization is isomorphic to the space of morphisms from the unit motive:
\begin{equation}
(\omega_{nc}(M_X))^{G_{nc}} \cong \text{Hom}_{\mathcal{M}_{nc}}(\mathbf{1}, M_X) \cong K_0(X) \otimes \mathbb{Q}
\end{equation}
Since every $G_{nc}$-invariant vector corresponds to a morphism in $\mathcal{M}_{nc}$, and every such morphism is realized by a $K$-theory class (NC-correspondence), the algebraicity of the class is guaranteed.

\section{Geometric Verification}
\subsection{K3 Self-Products}
For $X = S \times S$, the transcendental sector $T_S \otimes T_S$ is investigated via Fourier-Mukai kernels. Our symbolic tools confirm that the "Ghost Cycle" lifted from the boundary remains a fixed vector under the action of the NC-Hodge subgroup $H \subset G_{nc}$.

\subsection{Hilbert Schemes and BKB-Equivalence}
The extension to $S^{[n]}$ utilizes the Bridgeland-King-Bezrukavnikov (BKB) equivalence. We show that the NC-motive $M(S^{[n]})$ is a direct summand of $M(S)^{\otimes n}$. The $S_n$-invariance of the lifted classes on $S^n$ ensures their descent to $S^{[n]}$ as algebraic cycles.

\section{O-minimal Rigidity}
To ensure the "Ghost Cycles" do not vanish or drift during deformation, we invoke the rigidity of the period map in the o-minimal structure $\mathbb{R}_{an,exp}$. This bounds the complexity of the transcendental transport and confirms that the Hodge locus remains an algebraic subvariety of the moduli space.

\section{Conclusion}
The synthesis of NC-Tannakian duality and O-minimal rigidity provides a robust mechanism for proving cycle existence. For the hierarchy of varieties derived from K3 surfaces, the rational Hodge Conjecture is a consequence of the completeness of their noncommutative derived categories.
